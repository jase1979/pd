GCSE Design & Technology — Product Design

{{ _level.subtitle }}

This is the new WJEC specification, first taught from September 2026 with the first award in 2028. It is a different qualification from the one covered in the legacy GCSE section — the units, weightings and assessment have all changed, so the two are kept separate.

Learners choose one of three pathways — Engineering Design, Fashion & Textiles or Product Design — and must use the same pathway in both units. These pages cover the Product Design pathway, plus the core content that every learner studies whichever pathway they take.
