How the Qualification is Assessed

WJEC GCSE Design & Technology — teaching from 2026, first award 2028

The two units

Unit 1: Design & Technology in the 21st CenturyUnit 2: Design Project
TypeDigital (on-screen) examinationNon-examination assessment (NEA)
Length1 hour 30 minutesApproximately 40 hours
Marks80100
Weighting30%70%
Marked byWJECThe centre, externally moderated by WJEC

Unit 1 asks for objective responses, short answers and extended answers. It covers the core content plus the in-depth content for your chosen pathway.

Unit 2 is a sustained design and make task based on a contextual challenge set by WJEC, assessing your ability to identify, investigate, analyse and outline design possibilities, then design and make prototypes and evaluate their fitness for purpose.

Assessment objectives

AO1AO2AO3AO4Total
Unit 115%15%——30%
Unit 2—15%35%20%70%
Overall15%30%35%20%100%
  • AO1 — demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the principles, materials, tools and techniques used in design and technology.
  • AO2 — apply that knowledge and understanding.
  • AO3 — develop designs and tangible products.
  • AO4 — analyse and evaluate products and solutions in a range of design and technology contexts.

Notice that AO3 is the single largest slice of the qualification at 35%, and it is only available in Unit 2. Together with AO4, that makes the Design Project worth more than twice the exam.

How deeply you need to know something

The specification describes each topic using five stems, and they tell you what depth is expected:

StemWhat it means
Learners should knowBasic knowledge and understanding.
Learners should be able toApply knowledge to a practical situation, or demonstrate a practical skill.
Learners should understandGreater depth — apply to unfamiliar contexts, analyse and evaluate.
Learners should be aware ofYou do not need to know every aspect in detail.
Learners are required toA requirement of the unit itself.

Two words matter when reading the content lists. "Including" means the content must be taught and can be examined. "For example" or "such as" means the content is guidance only, and other examples are equally acceptable.
